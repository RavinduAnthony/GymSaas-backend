There are two separate member types when it comes to the payment management module
	1. Monthly package member - A
	2. Special package member - B

Member - A
* First do the registration fee and there is no late or regular pay scenarios for registration fee
* then for the initial payment part
	To identify the initial payment for a member, check whether any payment done by this member other than registration fee, If no then
	it is the initial pay 
* if the member done the initial payment in the 4th week of the month then that payment should be calculated for next month
* If member done his payment in 1st,2nd or 3rd week then the payment should be considered for current month
* After this initial payment for the upcoming months member should do the payment within 1st week of the month, then it is considered as a  regular payment
* if the member done the payment in 2nd, 3rd or 4th week it must be considered as a late payment

Member - B
* First the member do the registration fee as usual
* Then the member must do the full payment for selected package

* In these packages payment considered by the initial payment date and package duration
	ex: If the member do the initial payment on 2026-04-05 for 3 months package, then that amount is valid for package termination
on 2026-07-05

	1. Full payment
* if the user do the full payment for special package then the payment is considered as a full settled payment
